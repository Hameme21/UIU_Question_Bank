United International University (UIU) CSE Question Bank

Official open academic resource archive for UIU Computer Science & Engineering students

Welcome to UIU CSE Question Bank, the dedicated academic resource hub for undergraduate students of the Department of Computer Science and Engineering at United International University (United City, Madani Avenue, Badda, Dhaka 1212, Bangladesh). This platform assists students across all trimesters in preparing for university examinations by providing immediate access to verified midterm question papers, final exam question papers, detailed answer solutions, and comprehensive class lecture notes.

Whether you are preparing for introductory courses like Structured Programming Language (CSE 1111) and Object Oriented Programming (CSE 1115), mastering Data Structures & Algorithms (CSE 2215 / CSE 2217), or revising for core engineering subjects including Database Systems (CSE 3521), Operating Systems (CSE 3411), and Computer Networks (CSE 3711), our indexed archive helps you review past question trends, understand exam patterns, and achieve top grades.

Frequently Asked Questions (UIU Students FAQ)

What is UIU Question Bank?

UIU Question Bank is an open academic repository created for United International University (UIU) Computer Science & Engineering (CSE) students. It contains past midterm and final examination questions, verified solutions, and lecture notes organized by trimester, semester, and course.

How can I search and download UIU exam questions?

Students can search by course code (e.g., CSE 1111, CSE 2215) or course title in the search bar, filter by trimester or academic semester, and view or download PDF question papers directly.

Are solutions and lecture notes available for UIU courses?

Yes, verified solution sheets, step-by-step exam answers, and faculty lecture notes contributed by high-achieving students and instructors are available for download and preview.

How do I upload past question papers or solutions?

Click on the Upload tab in the navigation menu, select your course code, specify the examination type (Midterm or Final) and trimester, and submit your PDF document. All submissions are quickly reviewed and published.

Which courses and trimesters are covered in the question bank?

All 92 courses in the UIU CSE undergraduate curriculum across Trimester 1 through Trimester 12, as well as elective courses, are covered in the question bank.
